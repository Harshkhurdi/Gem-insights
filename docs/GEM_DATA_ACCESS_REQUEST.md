# Draft request for authorized GeM bulk data / API access

Prepared for the operator to review and send. **Not sent.** Official enquiry routes verified 19 October 2026: [GeM contact/help desk](https://gem.gov.in/contactUs) and [Integration Toolkit enquiry for non-registered users](https://gem.gov.in/home/dwn/user_info/ifms). The latter requests personal and organization details; no submission or access grant has been made. Request routing to the team responsible for data licensing/integrations rather than assuming that the toolkit supplies a public tender API.

**Subject: Request for authorized medical-equipment bid data access, historical coverage and research/commercial analytics permissions**

Sir,

I am developing GeM Intelligence, an independent procurement research application, initially focused on medical equipment tenders across India. Its intended purpose is to understand requested products and quantities, purchasing institutions, delivery regions and changes in procurement activity. It may be used for internal business research and, subject to your written terms, commercial analytical services. It will not represent tender announcements as completed purchases or claim complete market coverage without evidence.

Please advise whether GeM can provide an authorized API, bulk export, licensed feed or other approved data-sharing arrangement for this use. We would like to begin with a limited medical-equipment pilot and expand only within the scope you approve.

The requested fields are GeM bid identifiers, original titles and category information, publication/closing dates, buyer institution/department, requested product line items, quantities and units, explicitly published procurement estimates, actual consignee/delivery destinations, source document references, amendments, cancellations and available status history. We do not seek seller credentials, non-public bids, personal identifiers or protected evaluation information. Please identify fields that must be excluded or redacted.

Could you clarify:

1. Eligibility for private research/business users; application process, documentation, fees, contacts and any required agreement.
2. Available categories and geographic scope, identifier stability, update frequency, pagination/cursors, quotas, schema documentation, support and change notifications.
3. Whether historical bid data can be supplied, the earliest available date, archive retention, amendment/re-tender relationships, known gaps and a reliable completeness denominator.
4. Permissions to download and process authorized tender PDFs locally, extract factual line items, retain originals or only minimal evidence, store checksums/page references, and maintain audited historical source versions.
5. Rights to retain and reproduce tender text/documents, display source-linked facts, publish derived aggregate statistics, provide commercial reports and export data to authorized customers. Please distinguish factual-field/aggregate use from redistribution of complete documents or raw datasets, including any third-party rights.
6. Required attribution, acceptable-use restrictions, privacy/redaction controls, retention periods, deletion/revocation procedures and restrictions on storage location or subcontractors.
7. Whether a pilot dataset and an authorized test environment can be provided to validate extraction accuracy and source coverage before operational access.
8. Whether at least 50 representative original medical-equipment bid PDFs can be supplied with evaluation/annotation permission, including scans, bilingual formats, multi-product/consignee tables, amendments and service/accessory negatives. Please clarify rights to retain reviewed factual ground truth, short evidence and aggregate benchmark results when original retention is restricted.
9. Stable category, buyer and consignee identifiers; quantity/unit definitions; revision timestamps; cancellations; deletion/revocation notifications; incremental updates; and export exclusions. Please distinguish requested quantities/estimates from awards and contract values.

Processing would use local deterministic extraction and optional local OCR. Documents and extracted data would not be transmitted to external AI services. Administration would require authorization and human review, preserve provenance and corrections, obey your rate limits, and keep coverage limitations visible. We will not circumvent CAPTCHA, authentication, anti-bot controls or undocumented access restrictions.

Please confirm the approved access method and permitted uses in writing before any bulk collection or redistribution begins. If the Integration Toolkit serves a different integration purpose, please direct this request to the appropriate data-access/licensing team.

Regards,
Harsh Khurdi
GeM Intelligence project
